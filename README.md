# Sobrina Ballerina

*A magical ballet for the browser.*

You are a young ballerina on a warm, softly lit stage. Dance from spotlight to
spotlight using real ballet steps — plié, grand jeté, pirouette, arabesque —
and finish the performance beautifully.

There are no enemies, no damage, and nothing to defeat. The question the game
asks is not *can you survive the stage?* but **can you finish it beautifully?**

## Playing the Game

> **Play now:** [Launch Sobrina Ballerina](https://vibezzzcoder.github.io/sobrina-ballerina/)

Or alternatively, open **`release/upload/index.html`** in any modern browser. Double-click it,
or drag it into a browser window. That single file *is* the whole game.

**It works completely offline.** Airplane mode, no Wi-Fi, a plane, a car, a
waiting room — it all works exactly the same. The game never contacts the
internet: every drawing is packed inside that one file, all the music and
sound is generated by the game as it plays, and there are no fonts, servers,
accounts, ads, trackers, or downloads of any kind. Nothing you do is sent
anywhere, and nothing is saved to your device.

Works on desktop, tablet, and phone. Touch controls appear automatically on
touch devices.

## How to Dance

### On a keyboard

| What you want to do | Press |
| --- | --- |
| Choose a menu option | ← → to pick, then **Enter** |
| Walk | ← → or **A** / **D** |
| Plié — bend to prepare | Hold **↓** or **S** |
| Grand jeté — leap | Release the plié, or **Space** / **↑** while moving |
| Pirouette — turn | **Shift** or **J** |
| Spotting — snap your eyes back | **Space**, **↑**, **Shift** or **J** while the star flashes |
| Arabesque — hold your line | Hold **Space** on a glowing mark (**K** also works) |
| Final pose | Hold the action key in the last spotlight |
| Pause | **Esc** or **P** |
| Start over | **R** |
| Sound on / off | **M** |

### On a touchscreen

| What you want to do | Tap |
| --- | --- |
| Walk | **◀** **▶** |
| Plié, then leap | Hold **Leap**, then let go |
| Pirouette | **Twirl** |
| Spotting | Tap **anywhere** while the star flashes |
| Arabesque and final pose | Hold **Pose** |
| Pause | **❚❚** |
| Sound on / off | **♪** |

You only ever need three keys: **S** to gather, **Shift** to turn, **Space**
for every hold and every timing cue. The game draws each one as a key on
screen when you need it, so you never have to remember which is which.

All touch targets are large on purpose. Nothing in this game ever asks you to
tap a small or precise spot — the spotting cue accepts a tap anywhere on the
screen.

**Portrait** gives you a tall view that follows the dancer, with big controls
along the bottom. **Landscape** shows the whole stage at once. Both are fully
playable; turn your device whichever way feels better.

## The Steps

Each move is a real ballet step, used the way a dancer would use it.

**Plié** — a bend through the knees with the body held tall. It is how a
dancer prepares to jump. Hold it to gather power.

**Grand jeté** — a big travelling leap with both legs stretched. Released from
a good plié, it carries you across the shadow.

**Pirouette and spotting** — a turn on one leg. Spotting is the real technique
dancers use to keep from getting dizzy: your eyes stay fixed on one point,
then your head whips around last to find it again. When the star flashes,
that's your point — snap back to it.

**Arabesque** — standing tall on one leg with the other reaching long behind
you. Hold it steady on a glowing mark and let the line stretch.

## Poise, Not Health

There is no health bar. There is **Poise** — your confidence on stage.

Poise **grows** when you land cleanly in the light, time a step well, hold a
pose steadily, complete a phrase, or reach a stage mark.

Poise **fades** when you linger in shadow, land badly, miss a spotting cue, or
bump the moving curtain.

If Poise runs all the way out, you get **stage fright** — the lights soften,
the dancer gathers herself, and you are invited to take a breath and try
again. It is never a scary failure, and starting over is instant.

## Finishing Beautifully

To reach the finale you must actually dance the program: the grand jeté, the
travelling spotted turn, and the arabesque. Reaching the end of the stage is
not enough on its own. Once all three are done, hold the final pose for two
seconds in the last spotlight.

Then you're graded — gently. Three things are scored:

- **Poise** — how confident you stayed;
- **Timing** — how well you caught the spotting cues;
- **Lines** — your **Perfect Lines**, awarded for a properly prepared jeté at
  the top of its arc and for a steady arabesque held long in the light.

You'll get up to three stars, one sentence of specific praise, and one gentle
suggestion of what to focus on next. There are no letter grades and no red
Xs. Three stars are genuinely reachable — a clean run with both Perfect Lines
gets there.

## Two Ways to Dance

**Rehearse** teaches one step at a time. Each lesson explains what the ballet
idea *is*, shows you exactly which key or button to use, is forgiving with
Poise, and lets you retry just that step until it feels good. Nothing is
locked or lost.

**Opening Night** is the whole program, danced through without stopping.

You can move between them freely from the menus at any time.

## Comfort and Accessibility

- Text is large, high-contrast, and written in plain language. Every ballet
  term gets a friendly one-line explanation the first time it appears.
- No flashing or strobe effects — only brief, soft pulses.
- Colour is never the only signal; cues also change shape and motion.
- Timing windows are generous by design.
- Pause is instant and reliable, and the game pauses itself if you switch
  tabs or windows.
- Sound is entirely optional. Press **M** (or tap **♪**) any time. The game
  plays and looks exactly the same with sound off — even the gentle pulse of
  the stage, which is driven by the game's own clock rather than by the
  music.

## About the Music

Everything you hear is created by the game while you play — there are no
audio files. A warm 96 BPM score with piano and celesta, soft strings, a
gentle bass, light cuatro-like plucks, and a clave-shaped pulse, plus
applause when you finish. The stage lights breathe on that same pulse.
